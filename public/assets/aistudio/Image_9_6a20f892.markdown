## Brand & Style

The design system evolves into an ultra-modern aesthetic grounded in **Modern Swiss Horology and Quiet Luxury**. Moving decisively away from ornate, baroque serif traditions, the identity centers on austere architectural precision, razor-clean silhouettes, and quiet confidence. It addresses an elite tier of watch enthusiasts and minimalist connoisseurs who value craftsmanship, uncompromised materials, and technological clarity.

The visual direction combines **Architectural Minimalism and Frosted Monochromatic Depth**:
- **Deep Carbon Canvas:** Matte abyssal backdrops (`#0a0a0b` and `#121214`) absorb extraneous noise, creating an immaculate staging environment where watch photography, brushed bezels, and crystalline dials command undivided attention.
- **Micro-Machined Linear Detailing:** Heavy shadows and skeuomorphic weight are replaced by hairline platinum strokes (`border-white/10`), ultra-fine grid alignments, and frosted glass overlays that mirror anti-reflective sapphire crystal.
- **Editorial Sans-Serif Restraint:** Typography is stripped of historical flourishes. Clean grotesque forms with generous tracking and refined weights establish a balanced rhythm between industrial watchmaking and avant-garde editorial publishing.

## Colors

The color palette is strictly controlled, monochromatic, and metallic, engineered to emulate brushed steel, titanium, and smoked anti-reflective glass:

- **Primary (`#ffffff`):** Pure optical white, utilized with disciplined hierarchy for high-priority headlines, key interaction triggers, and active states.
- **Secondary (`#e2e8f0`):** Platinum steel. Provides refined metallic tonality for subheadings, active micro-borders, and tactile icon accents.
- **Tertiary (`#25d366`):** Concierge emerald. Strictly restricted to direct acquisition touchpoints (WhatsApp checkout and personal consultation triggers), ensuring immediate clarity without breaking the dark monochrome atmosphere.
- **Neutral (`#0a0a0b`):** Deep matte carbon canvas. Eliminates glare and sets a zero-reflection environment.
- **Named Colors:** Surface tiers step subtly through `#121214` and `#18181b`, punctuated by hairline borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) and muted slate text (`#94a3b8`) for technical indices.

## Typography

The type system is completely unified around **Geist**, a high-precision, contemporary geometric grotesque designed with pristine kerning, neutral geometry, and technical authority. Traditional serifs are eliminated entirely.

- **Weight Discipline:** Headlines employ light (`300`) to regular (`400`) weights with tighter negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`), achieving an architectural, understated luxury look rather than bulky commercial boldness.
- **Technical Indexing (`label-caps`):** Sub-labels, serial numbers, caliber details, and brand references use uppercase settings paired with expansive tracking (`0.18em`), recalling the meticulous engraved dials of modern independent Swiss watchmakers.
- **Body & Legibility:** Descriptive narrative and horological specifications leverage regular and light cuts (`300` and `400`), guaranteeing instant parsing across all display densities.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by an open 12-column architectural grid defined by generous negative space and surgical alignment:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** Outer canvas margins expand to `4rem` (capped at a 1440px reading container) with `1.5rem` gutters. Horological product grids conform to clean 3-column or 4-column balanced modules with ample interstitial breathing room.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** Adapts to an 8-column system with `2.5rem` margin and `1.25rem` gutter, utilizing 2-column card distributions.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Collapses to a 4-column framework with `1.25rem` margin. Showcases transition to single-column focus cards or edge-to-edge touch carousels.

Spacing strictly follows mathematical increments where macro-negative space acts as an active visual element, signaling calm luxury and effortless confidence.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through **Smoked Glass Surfaces and Hairline Specular Borders**, completely rejecting heavy, muddy drop shadows:

- **Base Canvas:** Matte carbon `#0a0a0b`.
- **Containers & Modules:** Layered with subtle smoked glass (`background: rgba(18, 18, 20, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- **Hairline Specular Borders:** Cards, modals, and input fields define their boundaries exclusively via crisp 1px borders of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Specular Hover State:** Interactive surfaces elevate not by moving upward or casting diffuse dark shadows, but by illuminating their perimeter line to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.22)` accompanied by an imperceptible internal glow (`inset 0 0 12px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft Linear Geometry (`roundedness: 1`)**:

- Cards, action buttons, dialogs, and input containers maintain an exacting `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius. This honors the beveled facets, lugs, and geometric cases of modern avant-garde timepieces without falling into clinical sharpness.
- Status badges and technical tags scale down to an ultra-refined `0.125rem` (2px) radius.
- Circular rounding (`rounded-full`) is isolated strictly to circular dial indicators, status indicators, and image carousel pagers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Linear Button:** Clean white solid background (`#ffffff`), pitch carbon text (`#0a0a0b`), 4px border radius, set in `label-caps` (`Geist`, medium weight, tracking `0.15em`). On hover, transitions cleanly to platinum (`#e2e8f0`) with subtle specular glow.
- **Ghost Linear Button:** Pure transparent or smoked base with a 1px boundary (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), platinum text (`#e2e8f0`). On hover, border brightens to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)` with an internal fill of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- **Concierge CTA:** Minimalist deep slate base (`#0f1712`) with a hairline emerald boundary (`rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.35)`) and `#25d366` typography, maintaining low-noise luxury while facilitating fast mobile conversion.

### Timepiece Showcase Cards
- **Image Chamber:** Pristine 1:1 or 4:5 frame set against a dark smoked backdrop (`rgba(18, 18, 20, 0.6)`), devoid of harsh vignettes, prioritizing authentic sapphire reflections and case grain.
- **Spec Grid & Metadata:** Model reference and collection name set in `label-caps` (`#94a3b8`), model name rendered in light-weight `headline-sm` (`#ffffff`), followed by minimalist tabular data (diameter, caliber, power reserve) formatted cleanly with hairline dividers.
- **Linear Footers:** Direct link actions integrated as flush, borderless button rows with subtle micro-arrow transitions on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Micro-proportioned rectangular badges (`0.125rem` radius) utilizing a translucent carbon background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`) with a 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) and platinum uppercase micro-typography (`10px`, tracking `0.18em`).

### Input Fields
- Understated architectural inputs: Solid `#121214` surface with a 1px perimeter border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`). Placeholder text in muted slate (`#94a3b8`). On focus, the border transitions smoothly to crisp platinum (`#e2e8f0`) without colored drop-shadow glows.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Minimal square controls (4px radius) with hairline platinum borders. Active states fill with solid `#ffffff` and a carbon glyph, delivering immediate tactile feedback through minimal means.
